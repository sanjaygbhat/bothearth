## Is this open source?

The source is available, but the present noncommercial restriction means this is **not OSI open source**. The [Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd) requires permission for business use as well as other fields of endeavor. Public source availability and a zero software price do not remove the restriction.

## What might still cost money?

- Your model provider's subscription, API calls, or extra usage.
- Your own computer, electricity, connectivity, and any separately licensed container runtime.
- An optional VPS, domain, or notification service you choose yourself.

BotHearth does not include those services. Its task meter estimates usage and cannot cap third-party bills; configure spending controls at the provider. See [provider requirements](docs/PROVIDERS.md).
