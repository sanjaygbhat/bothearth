01 / A separate computer

Give it a place to work.

A Chromium browser with its own profile. A separate shell container. A configured workspace on your host. Your everyday browser profile is not mounted by default.

02 / A human in the loop

Step in when it matters.

Review new destinations and detected sensitive actions. During human control, model capture is blocked. Approval detection has limits; stay present for sensitive work.

03 / A result to inspect

Keep the work in view.

Follow task steps, review requests, and open saved outputs. Task allowances can pause work. Your provider's own limits and charges still apply.
