Your host stores the primary workspace, task records and browser profiles. Remote models still receive the context you submit. Know the boundary before handing over a task.

YOUR INFRASTRUCTURE

Keep the workspace with you.

Run on your Mac, Linux computer or a prepared Linux VM. Choose the host, storage, network access and backups. BotHearth does not require its own hosted agent account.

YOUR MODEL ACCESS

Bring the connection you choose.

Use a supported model account through its native CLI or API adapter. Provider terms, processing and charges still apply; self-hosting is not a promise of zero data egress.

YOUR CONTROLS

Watch, review and take over.

See the browser, review detected sensitive actions, and pause or stop work. During acknowledged human control, model capture and ordinary agent actions are blocked.
